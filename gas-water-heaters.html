What Are Gas Water Heaters?

Gas water heaters use natural gas or propane to heat water quickly and efficiently, making them a popular choice for residential and commercial properties.

Gas water heaters work by burning natural gas or propane in a burner at the bottom of the tank. The heat rises through the tank, warming the water. A thermostat monitors the temperature and turns the burner on when needed. These systems are known for their rapid recovery rate, meaning they can heat water faster than electric models.

Whether you have a traditional tank-style gas water heater or are considering an upgrade, our team has the expertise to handle all your gas water heater needs safely and professionally.

Benefits of Gas Water Heaters

Faster Heating

Gas heats water significantly faster than electric, providing hot water when you need it most.

Lower Operating Costs

Natural gas is typically less expensive than electricity, saving you money on monthly bills.

Works During Outages

Gas water heaters continue to function during power outages, ensuring consistent hot water.

High Recovery Rate

Reheats water quickly, perfect for large families or high-demand situations.

Important Safety Information

Gas water heaters require proper installation and maintenance to operate safely. Always ensure:

  • Professional installation by licensed technicians
  • Proper ventilation to prevent carbon monoxide buildup
  • Regular inspection of gas connections and pilot light
  • Clearance around the unit per manufacturer specifications
  • Working carbon monoxide detectors in your home

If you smell gas, leave immediately and call your gas company from outside your home.
