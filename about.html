About Mr. WaterHeater

Our Story

Mr. WaterHeater was founded with a simple mission: to provide Palm Beach County residents with reliable, affordable, and expert water heater services they can trust.

Over the years, we've grown from a small local operation to one of the most trusted names in water heater installation, repair, and maintenance in the region. Our success is built on three core principles:

  • Quality First: We use only the best products and materials, ensuring every installation meets the highest standards.
  • Customer-Focused: Your satisfaction is our top priority. We listen to your needs and provide honest, transparent advice.
  • Expert Service: Our licensed technicians bring years of experience and stay current with the latest technology.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of satisfied customers throughout Palm Beach County, delivering the same commitment to excellence that defined our first day in business.
